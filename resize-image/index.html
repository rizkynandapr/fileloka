Resize images to exact pixels or a percentage

Scale pictures to the size a profile photo, marketplace or form asks for. Keep the proportions automatically or set exact width and height, for one image or a whole batch.

How it works

  1. Pick the images you want to resize, or drop them on the page.
  2. Type a width, a height or both in pixels. Or switch to percent.
  3. Resize and download. The new size is added to each file name.

Questions about this tool

How do I resize without stretching the image?

Leave 'Keep proportions' on. Enter only a width (or only a height) and the other side is worked out for you. If you enter both, the image is fitted inside that box without distortion.

Can I make an image bigger?

Yes, both modes can upscale. Browsers smooth the result nicely, but anything much past 2 times will look soft. That's just how enlarging works.

Does resizing reduce quality?

Shrinking keeps images crisp. JPGs are saved at 92 percent quality, which you won't notice on photos. PNGs stay lossless.

Are my pictures uploaded?

No. Resizing happens in your browser's canvas on your device.
