Convert images between JPG, PNG and WebP

Change formats in one click. WebP screenshots to JPG so they open everywhere, JPG to PNG for editing, anything to WebP for smaller web images. Works in batches and nothing gets uploaded.

Questions about this tool

Which format should I pick?

JPG for photos and anything that has to open everywhere. PNG for screenshots, logos and transparency. WebP when you want the smallest files for a website. If you're not sure, JPG is the safe choice.

What happens to transparency in a JPG?

JPG can't store transparency, so transparent areas get a white background. We'd rather tell you now than surprise you with black boxes.

Why can't I choose WebP?

Some browsers, mostly older Safari, can't save WebP. When that's the case the option is hidden and a note explains why. JPG and PNG work everywhere.

Is the conversion done on a server?

No. Your browser re-encodes the images on your device, which is why it feels instant.
