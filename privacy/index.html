Privacy, in plain words

Short version: your files never leave your device, and we don't know who you are.

Your files

Every tool runs in your browser. The file you pick is read into your device's memory, processed there, and gone when you close the tab. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, so we couldn't look at it, keep it or hand it over even if someone asked.

You can check this yourself. Open your browser's developer tools, watch the Network tab while a tool runs, and you'll see no request carrying your file. Or load a page, switch off your internet, and run the tool anyway.

What we count

To know which tools are worth improving, the site counts a few events: a page was opened, a tool finished a job, a result was downloaded, a link in the support box was clicked. Each count carries the page path, the tool name, the page language, your country as reported by our host, and whether the device is a phone or a computer.

It does not carry your IP address, a cookie, an ID that follows you around, a file name, a file size or anything from inside a file. Counts go to our own address (fileloka.id/api/e), not to an analytics company, and they can't be traced back to a person.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is counted.

Cookies and storage

Fileloka sets no cookies and saves nothing in your browser. That's also why the dark mode switch goes back to your system setting when you reload.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles technical request data such as IP addresses in order to deliver pages and block abuse. That handling falls under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Ads and sponsors

There are no third-party ads on Fileloka today.
